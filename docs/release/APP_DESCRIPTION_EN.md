## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

<!-- FULL_EN_START -->
Hisn Al-Muslim is a calm, beautifully designed companion for your daily remembrance of Allah — and it works completely offline.

It brings together the adhkar and supplications of the well-known book "Hisn al-Muslim min Adhkar al-Kitab was-Sunnah" (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf al-Qahtani, together with supplications from the verses of the Quran, organised into clear categories with the source of each dhikr.

◆ Complete categories
• Morning • Evening • Before sleeping • Upon waking
• After prayer • Prayer • Mosque & adhan
• Food & drink • Home & daily life • Travel
• Supplications from the Quran • Prophetic supplications • Various occasions

◆ Comfortable reading
• Clear Arabic typography; Quranic verses in a Mushaf-style typeface
• Repetition counter showing what's left, with auto-advance
• Adjustable text size and optional English translation
• The book's footnote (reference and virtue) for each dhikr where available
• Copy, share and favourite with one tap

◆ Daily wird and saved progress
• Track your morning and evening adhkar every day
• A streak of consecutive days to help you stay consistent
• Your place is saved automatically

◆ Digital tasbeeh
• A large, responsive counter with optional vibration
• Targets of 33, 100, 1000 or no limit, with rounds and statistics
• Ready-made phrases and your own custom dhikr

◆ Instant search
• Search any word — diacritics are optional
• Searches the Arabic text, occasions and English translations

◆ Optional reminders
• Morning, evening and bedtime reminders plus your own
• Notification permission is requested only when you turn a reminder on

◆ Privacy first
• Works offline and does not request internet access
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays on your device

◆ Refined design
• Full Arabic right-to-left interface and an English interface
• Light and dark themes
• Supports large text and screen readers

Sources: adhkar text from the official Hisn al-Muslim website (hisnmuslim.com); Quranic text from Tanzil (tanzil.net); English meaning of the Quranic supplications: Saheeh International. The content was carefully matched with the book's footnotes — we welcome your feedback to correct any mistake.
