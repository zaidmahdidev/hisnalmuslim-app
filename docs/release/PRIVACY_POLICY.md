## English

Hisn Al-Muslim ("the app") is an offline collection of adhkar and
supplications. We designed it to need no personal data.

**Data we collect:** none. The app does not collect, store on our servers,
sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no account system, no
advertising, no analytics and no tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. The app
does not request internet access.

**Data stored on your device:** your favorites, reading progress, tasbeeh
counts, reminder times and settings are saved locally on your device so the
app works offline. They never leave the device through the app. You can
delete them at any time in *Settings → Clear all app data* or by uninstalling
the app. If your device's own backup (e.g. Android backup or iCloud) is
enabled, the operating system may include this local data in your personal
backup; that backup is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

**Notifications:** if you turn on a reminder, the app asks for the
notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. No
notification data is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app or
in your device settings.

**Sharing and copying:** when you choose to copy or share a dhikr, the text
is passed to the clipboard or to the app you select. We do not receive it.

**Children:** the app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from
anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be published at
this URL with a new effective date.
